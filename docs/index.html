BetterAttend

An unofficial Android and iOS app for Hack Club Attend. Check people in, scan tickets and look after travel at your event, including when there's no signal.

Check-in

  • qr_code_scanner

    Scanning

    Continuous QR scanning with the camera, plus NFC badges. Results show safety alerts and can be undone.

  • cloud_sync

    Works offline

    Scans made without signal are queued and synced later. Rosters and tickets are cached on the phone.

  • tablet_android

    Kiosk mode

    Self check-in on a spare phone or tablet, locked with a PIN.

11:30

People

  • group

    Search and filter

    Search and filter everyone at the event. Each person's page starts with their safety alerts.

  • campaign

    Announcements

    Send a Slack message to everyone at the event and see how many were delivered.

  • share

    Share stats

    Hold any number on Home to share it as an image.

Travel

  • flight_land

    Arrivals and pickups

    Arrivals and departures by day, with flight status and who has been picked up.

  • widgets

    Widgets

    Check-ins, arrivals, quick scan and your next ticket on the home screen.

11:30

Tickets

  • confirmation_number

    Offline passes

    Participants' passes open offline, and the screen brightens for the QR code.

  • wallet

    Google Wallet

    Add your pass to Google Wallet, or Apple Wallet on iOS.

  • login

    Same account

    Sign in with your existing Attend account. It installs next to the official app.

Download

The latest release has APKs for Android 8.0 and newer. Most phones need arm64-v8a, and universal works on any phone. Obtainium can install it from GitHub and keep it updated.
