For Hack Club event organisers

The Attend app, rebuilt for the door.

BetterAttend is a faster, offline-first version of Hack Club Attend. Scan tickets, check people in, meet arrivals and keep an eye on everyone, with or without signal.

Scan

Built for the front door.

Point the camera and keep going. BetterAttend scans continuously, reads NFC badges, and shows a big colour-coded result with any safety alerts before you wave someone through.

  • Scans made without signal queue up and sync on their own
  • Undo, sounds and haptics, and a log of recent scans
  • Find person for manual check-ins when a QR code won't load
11:30

People

Know who's in the room.

Search everyone by name, email, pronouns or short code, and filter by who's here, who isn't, and who needs attention.

  • Safety alerts first: allergies, medication, unaccompanied minors
  • Call, text, WhatsApp or email in one tap
  • Write NFC badges and leave notes with a sensitivity level
11:30

Travel

Meet every arrival.

Every flight, train and bus, grouped by day in the event's timezone, with live flight status and who still needs collecting.

  • Mark people picked up straight from the airport
  • Unaccompanied minors flagged on every trip
  • An Arrivals widget shows who to collect next
11:30

Tickets

A ticket that works without signal.

Participants get their pass offline, with a high-contrast QR code that turns the screen brightness up when it's time to scan.

  • Add it to Google Wallet
  • Countdown, directions and messages from organisers
  • Your travel and safety contacts in one place
11:30

Also included

The rest of the event, covered.

Share the numbers

Press and hold any number on Home to turn it into an image card. Pick the colour, style and layout, then post it.

Kiosk mode

Turn a spare phone or tablet into a self check-in station, pinned and locked with a PIN.

Announcements

Message everyone at the event on Slack and watch it deliver.

Home-screen widgets

Check-in progress, arrivals, quick scan and your next ticket, in your wallpaper's colours.

Always current

Counts refresh in the background, and everything you've loaded still reads offline.

No tracking

No analytics or session recording. Cached data is encrypted on your phone.

Get BetterAttend

Sign in with the Hack Club account you already use for Attend. It installs alongside the official app. Most phones want the arm64-v8a APK, and universal runs on everything.
