BetterAttend

A faster, offline-first Hack Club Attend app for Android. Scan tickets, check people in, meet arrivals and run your whole event from your phone.

Download APK

Android 8.0+ · free and open source · signs in with your existing Attend account

Works without signal

Scans made offline are queued and synced automatically. Rosters and tickets are cached on the phone.

Built for the door

Continuous QR scanning, NFC badges, big colour-coded results, safety alerts, undo and a kiosk mode for self check-in.

Everything in one place

People, travel and pickups, announcements, home-screen widgets and shareable stat cards.

Also on iOS: the latest release includes an unsigned .ipa for AltStore or SideStore.

BetterAttend is an unofficial app, not made by Hack Club. Source on GitHub.
